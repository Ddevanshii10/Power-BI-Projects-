# Power BI Data Visualization & Dashboard Projects

Welcome to the **Power BI Projects** repository! This repository serves as a professional portfolio showcasing end-to-end data visualization reports, interactive business intelligence dashboards, and analytical reporting solutions built using **Microsoft Power BI**, **DAX**, and **Data Modeling**.

---

## 📊 Projects Overview

### 1. Car Sales Analysis Dashboard
- **Files:** `CAR SALES ANALYSIS.pbix`, `CAR SALES ANALYSIS.pbit`, `CAR SALES ANALYSIS.pdf`
- **Objective:** Analyze automotive sales metrics, track sales performance over time, and evaluate body style and transmission trends to provide actionable commercial insights.
- **Key Features & Metrics:**
  - **Sales KPI Tracking:** Overall Sales Revenue, Average Price, Total Cars Sold, and Year-over-Year (YoY) Growth.
  - **Trend & Seasonality Analysis:** Monthly and weekly sales performance analysis.
  - **Breakdowns:** Sales by Dealer Region, Body Style (Sedan, SUV, Hatchback, etc.), and Engine/Transmission Type.
  - **Export Formats:** Includes a Power BI Template (`.pbit`) for modular reusability and a static PDF export for quick report viewing.

### 2. E-Commerce Business 360 Dashboard
- **Files:** `E commerce Business 360 Dashbaord.pbix`, `E commerce Business 360 Dashbaord.pdf`
- **Objective:** Provide a 360-degree interactive view of e-commerce performance to optimize revenue operations, customer segmentation, and order fulfillment tracking.
- **Key Features & Metrics:**
  - **Revenue & Financial KPIs:** Total Revenue, Profit Margin, Total Orders, and Average Order Value (AOV).
  - **Customer & Product Insights:** Top-performing product categories, customer segmentation, and order frequency patterns.
  - **Operations & Delivery:** Order completion rates, delivery channel distribution, and regional revenue mapping.

### 3. Hotel Revenue Analysis
- **Files:** `Hotel Revenue Analysis.pbix`, `Hotel Revenue Analysis.pdf`
- **Objective:** Evaluate hotel booking performance, occupancy trends, and revenue generation strategies to optimize pricing and distribution channels.
- **Key Features & Metrics:**
  - **Revenue & Booking KPIs:** ADR (Average Daily Rate), RevPAR (Revenue Per Available Room), Total Revenue, and Cancellation Rates.
  - **Occupancy & Demographics:** Customer type breakdown, seasonal booking patterns, and lead time distribution.
  - **Market Segment Analysis:** Revenue performance across direct bookings, OTAs, and corporate segments.

### 4. Real Estate Analytics Dashboard
- **Files:** `real estate.pbix`, `real estate.pdf`
- **Objective:** Analyze property pricing trends, market distribution, and property feature valuation to support real estate investment decisions.
- **Key Features & Metrics:**
  - **Property Valuation KPIs:** Average Price per Sq Ft, Total Sales Volume, and Median Property Price.
  - **Geographic & Feature Breakdown:** Price distribution by location/neighborhood, property type, and bedroom/bathroom count.
  - **Market Dynamics:** Days on market and price-to-rent yield ratios.

### 5. Fraud Detection Analysis
- **Files:** `Fraud detection analysis.pdf`
- **Objective:** Identify anomalous transaction behavior, assess financial risk exposure, and visualize key risk indicators across transaction types.
- **Key Features & Metrics:**
  - **Risk Identification:** Transaction anomaly rate, total flagged loss value, and high-risk user flags.
  - **Pattern Analysis:** Geographic location risk mapping, channel distribution (online vs. in-store), and peak fraud time slots.

### 6. New Zealand Export Report
- **Files:** `NZ Export Report.pdf`
- **Objective:** Explore trade flow patterns, key export commodities, and international trading partner dynamics for New Zealand.
- **Key Features & Metrics:**
  - **Trade Volume KPIs:** Total Export Value, Top Export Partners, and Year-over-Year Trade Growth.
  - **Commodity Breakdown:** Export performance across key sectors (Agriculture, Dairy, Forestry, and Manufacturing).

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used

- **Business Intelligence Tool:** Microsoft Power BI Desktop
- **Data Transformation & Prep:** Power Query (M Language)
- **Calculations & Analytics:** DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) for measures, dynamic KPIs, and calculated tables
- **Data Modeling:** Star Schema / Snowflake Schema design
- **Report Formats:** `.pbix` (Power BI Workbooks), `.pbit` (Power BI Templates), `.pdf` (Exported Reports)
